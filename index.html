The challenge

Think ahead. Move with precision.

Plan your route, avoid every red node, and never cross the same segment twice. You may lift your finger whenever needed, but the same curve must continue precisely from the hand marker.

01

Draw one curve

Create a single continuous path through the geometric figure. Lift your finger when needed and resume only from the marked endpoint.

02

Cross every line once

Each segment must be crossed exactly one time. Do not trace along a line and do not touch it tangentially.

03

Avoid red nodes

Red nodes are off limits. Find a route with enough clearance to remain accurate from the first move to the last.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Kross app for iPhone or iPad and this official Kross website.

Effective date: September 11, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 11, 2026

In plain language: Kross requires no account and uses no developer-operated gameplay server. Your progress is stored locally on your device. The free version uses Google AdMob for advertising, Apple processes Premium purchases, and the website host may process standard technical logs.

1. Who is responsible

Kross is independently developed and published (referred to as “Kross,” “we,” “us,” or “our” in this policy). For privacy questions or requests, contact tavistudio@yahoo.com.

This policy applies to the Kross iOS and iPadOS app and to this website. It does not replace the separate privacy policies of Google, Apple, GitHub, or other services linked from this page.

2. Information Kross handles

Kross does not require account registration. The developer does not operate a remote database or backend for gameplay, does not receive your drawing paths or puzzle attempts, and does not add independent analytics or profiling tools.

The current version of Kross does not request access to your precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data. It does not ask for Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission. Third-party advertising services may still process identifiers and technical data available under applicable platform rules, device settings and your privacy choices, as explained below.

3. Advertising, Google AdMob and consent

The free version of Kross uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to provide interstitial ads and rewarded ads. A rewarded ad may be offered before a hint; interstitial ads may be shown only at configured gameplay intervals. Ads are not requested for a recognized Premium user.

Depending on your region, device settings, consent choices, ad configuration and the ad that is delivered, Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a general or approximate location;
  • device or app-bounded identifiers and, where law and platform permission allow, an advertising identifier;
  • advertising data, such as ads shown and interactions with ads;
  • product interactions, such as app launches, taps or video views related to advertising;
  • diagnostic information, crash logs, performance information and similar technical data.

Google and participating vendors may use this information to deliver and measure ads, limit repetition, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain security, diagnose problems, produce aggregated reporting and—where you have made the required choice—personalize advertising.

Consent and ad-serving modes

Kross uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). At launch, the app asks UMP whether a privacy message is required. When required, Kross presents Google’s form before enabling gameplay and checks whether ads may be requested. The form can cover consent under European privacy rules and choices under applicable U.S. state laws. Where Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Kross makes Manage Consent available from the in-app Privacy page.

Depending on applicable law and your choices, Google may provide personalized ads, non-personalized ads, limited ads or technical ad delivery. Choosing not to consent to personalized advertising does not necessarily mean that no ads will appear; contextual, limited or technical ads may still be served where permitted.

Kross does not independently decide which consent form or vendor list applies to a particular user. Google UMP determines whether a message is required based on its configuration and the information available to the SDK.

6. How information is used and disclosed

Information is used only as needed to operate Kross, remember progress, manage advertising frequency and consent, provide hints, verify Premium access, respond to support requests, maintain security and comply with legal obligations.

The developer does not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed or made available in these limited situations:

  • to Google and its participating vendors for advertising, consent management, measurement, security and related services in the free version;
  • to Apple for App Store distribution, purchases, restoration, refunds and fraud prevention;
  • to the website and email service providers needed to deliver the site and respond to your messages;
  • when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, enforceable government requests, protect rights or safety, investigate abuse, or defend legal claims;
  • as part of a legitimate transfer of the app or business, subject to appropriate notice and continued protection.

For laws that distinguish a “sale” from “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising, the developer does not sell your information for money. AdMob’s use or disclosure of information for advertising may be treated as “sharing” under some U.S. state laws. Where required, the Google privacy form provides the applicable choice.

7. Legal bases

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR or similar law applies, information may be handled on one or more of these bases:

  • Consent, for personalized advertising, device storage/access or related processing where consent is required. You can withdraw or change consent through the available in-app privacy options.
  • Performance of a contract, when necessary to provide gameplay features or the Premium purchase you request.
  • Legitimate interests, such as maintaining app security, preventing fraud, providing support and operating a reliable service, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation, where records or disclosures are required by applicable law.

Google, Apple and other independent providers determine their own legal bases for processing under their respective policies and consent interfaces.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Local game and ad-cadence data remains on your device until it is reset, the app’s data is removed or the app is deleted, subject to any device backup and restore behavior controlled by Apple.
  • Premium purchase records are retained by Apple under its legal and business requirements so that purchases can be verified or restored.
  • Google advertising and consent data is retained under Google’s and participating vendors’ policies.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, maintain an appropriate support record, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations.

Because local gameplay data is not uploaded to a developer server, we cannot view or remotely delete it. You can remove it from the device by deleting the app and, where applicable, its associated backup. This does not cancel or erase a purchase record held by Apple.
